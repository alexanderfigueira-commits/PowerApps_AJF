HOW TO USE THIS TRANSCRIPT
==========================

The talk runs about 8 minutes across 10 slides, roughly 45 seconds per slide. Read it aloud three times before the meeting: once slowly for meaning, once with the marks, once standing up with the slides.

  /  ->  Short pause, about one second
  //  ->  Long pause, two seconds; let the point land
  **word**  ->  Stress this word; say it a little louder and slower
  (look up)  ->  Look at the audience, not the screen
  (click)  ->  Move to the next slide

Rehearsal routine, 15 minutes a day for three days:

1. Record yourself on your phone reading slides 1 to 10.
2. Listen once and mark where you rushed or dropped your voice at the end of a sentence.
3. Read again, finishing every sentence with a steady, downward tone.
4. On day three, present from the cheat sheet at the end, not the full script.

TONE GUIDE: CONFIDENT AND OPEN
==============================

Confident means you speak about the work as something you know well. Open means you invite the audience in and welcome their view. You need both: confidence without openness sounds closed, openness without confidence sounds unsure.

  "We built…", "This means…"  ->  "We tried to…", "I think maybe…"
  Short sentences, then a pause  ->  Long sentences joined with "and… and…"
  End sentences on a falling tone  ->  Rising at the end, which sounds like a question
  "Let me show you…"  ->  "Sorry, just quickly…"
  "That's a good point; here's how it works today."  ->  "No, that's not right."
  "I'll check and come back to you by Friday."  ->  "I don't know."

Ready-made phrases:

- **Opening:** "Thank you for your time. In the next eight minutes I'll show you what we built and why it matters."
- **Moving on:** "So that's the why. Now let's look at the how." / "Here's where it gets practical."
- **Owning a decision:** "We chose this on purpose, because…"
- **Inviting input:** "I'd really value your view on this." / "What would make this more useful for your team?"
- **Buying time:** "Good question. Let me make sure I understand it: you're asking about…"
- **Not knowing:** "I don't have that figure with me. I'll send it to you after the meeting."
- **Closing:** "Your feedback shapes the next version. Thank you."

LIKELY QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS
============================

Start every answer with the short answer, then one reason. Thank people for a hard question; it shows you are open, not defensive. Check the answers about rollout and maintenance against your actual plans before the meeting, and adjust any that aren't decided yet.

  When can we start using it?  ->  "It's ready for user testing now. We go live once testing is done and the users are trained. I'll share the dates as soon as the testing group is confirmed."
  Who can see what?  ->  "Each person sees what their role allows. Requestors see their own requests; administrators see every submitted request and the review tools."
  Where is the data stored?  ->  "In our own SharePoint lists, inside Microsoft 365. Nothing leaves our environment."
  What happens to the old way of working?  ->  "We move deposits into the app step by step, so nobody is left without a channel during the change."
  Can it handle large files?  ->  "Visuals and documents are attached in the app. For very large video or audio files we keep the FTP path, which the form asks for."
  What if someone forgets a mandatory field?  ->  "They can't miss it: the field turns yellow until it's filled, and the validation step lists what's still needed."
  How do people know something changed?  ->  "The dashboards show it first, and e-mail notifications tell them when a status changes or a request is handed over."
  Can we add more production types or fields later?  ->  "Yes. The app is built to be extended, and your feedback decides what comes next."
  Who maintains it?  ->  "It runs on standard Microsoft 365 tools that we already support, and the app is fully documented."
  A question you can't answer  ->  "That's a good question, and I want to give you an accurate answer. Let me check and come back to you by [day]."
